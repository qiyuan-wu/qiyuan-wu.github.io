title: APS DFD 2026
---

I really enjoyed this year's APS Division of Fluid Dynamics meeting.

Two sessions I especially liked were
[Biological Fluid Dynamics: Invertebrate Swimming](https://dfd-meeting.aps.org/dfd/2026/events/F22)
on Monday and
[Free-Surface Flows: General](https://dfd-meeting.aps.org/dfd/2026/events/M29)
on Tuesday.

My favorite talks were Daire and Graham's on wave-driven propulsion, back to
back in the free-surface session:
[1D modelling and optimisation](https://dfd-meeting.aps.org/dfd/2026/events/M29/1)
and
[2D considerations and future perspectives](https://dfd-meeting.aps.org/dfd/2026/events/M29/2).
